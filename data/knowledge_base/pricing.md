# Pricing Guide

PulseIQ is priced as a monthly subscription based on lab size — the number of active
researchers using the platform. All tiers include core LIMS, analytics, and the AI
knowledge assistant. Advanced features scale with tier.

---

## Subscription Tiers

### Starter — Startup Biotech
**Target:** Early-stage biotech and research labs with under 50 active researchers.
Typically pre-Series B, discovery or early preclinical stage.

**Price:** $2,000/month (up to 25 users) or $2,500/month (26-50 users)
Billed annually. Month-to-month available at $2,400/$3,000/month respectively.

**Included:**
- Core LIMS (sample management, inventory, results tracking)
- Standard reporting and dashboards
- AI knowledge assistant (RAG-based Q&A on your documentation)
- Up to 3 instrument integrations
- Email support, 24-hour response SLA
- 500 GB data storage
- Standard onboarding (self-serve + 2 onboarding calls)

**Not included:**
- 21 CFR Part 11 validation documentation (available as add-on: $5,000)
- HL7/FHIR integration
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager
- Custom integrations
- SSO/SAML (available as add-on: $500/month)

## Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

**What counts as an "active user"?**
Any user who logs in at least once in a given calendar month. Read-only users (e.g.,
executives viewing dashboards) count at 50% of the per-user rate.

**Is there a free trial?**
We do not offer a self-serve free trial for enterprise software. We offer a structured
demo and, where appropriate, a paid POC engagement. This ensures every evaluation is
supported and gives accurate signal on fit.

**Can we start on Starter and upgrade later?**
Yes. Upgrades between tiers are available at any point. When upgrading, you pay the
prorated difference for the remainder of your contract term. All data and integrations
carry over automatically.

**Are professional services fees included?**
Standard onboarding is included in all tiers. Complex implementations, custom integrations
beyond the included allotment, and validation execution services are quoted separately by
our professional services team.
