# Competitor Battlecards

This document covers PulseIQ's three most commonly encountered competitors in life sciences
sales cycles. Use these battlecards to prepare for competitive deals and handle comparisons
during demos and negotiations.

---

## Competitor 1: LabMatrix Pro

**Company overview:** LabMatrix Pro is a legacy LIMS vendor founded in 2003. Strong in
large pharma, known for deep instrument integrations but slow product development cycles.
Typically surfaces in deals at companies with 200+ researchers who already have a LabMatrix
ELN deployment.

### Head-to-Head Comparison

| Dimension | PulseIQ | LabMatrix Pro |
|---|---|---|
| FDA 21 CFR Part 11 | Full compliance, validation docs included | Compliant, but validation docs sold separately ($15,000+) |
| Implementation timeline | 6-10 weeks | 16-24 weeks |
| AI-powered insights | Yes — daily autonomous insight agent | No — static reporting only |
| Lab instrument integrations | 40+ pre-built connectors | 80+ connectors (legacy advantage) |
| Pricing (mid-size pharma) | ~$6,000/month | ~$8,500/month + implementation fees |
| Cloud-native | Yes (AWS, GCP, Azure) | Partial — core is on-premise with cloud add-on |
| Mobile access | Full mobile support | Limited, desktop-focused |
| Customer support | Dedicated CSM, 4-hour SLA | Tiered support, 24-hour SLA standard |

### Where We Win
- Deals where speed to implementation matters (clinical deadlines, commercial launches)
- Mid-size biotechs that don't need 80+ legacy instrument connectors
- Any deal where AI-powered analytics is a differentiator
- Cloud-first organizations that don't want on-premise infrastructure

### Where They Win
- Large pharma with existing LabMatrix ELN investments (switching cost is high)
- Organizations with niche legacy instrument integrations we don't yet support
- Deals where procurement prefers established 20-year-old vendors

### Trap Questions to Ask the Prospect
- "Have you received a quote for LabMatrix's validation documentation package?"
- "What is their projected go-live date, and what happens if they miss it?"
- "Does your team have capacity to manage a 6-month implementation?"

## Competitor 2: Veridian BioAnalytics

**Company overview:** Veridian is a newer entrant (founded 2019) focused on AI-powered
lab analytics. Well-funded, aggressive on pricing, strong in genomics accounts. Their
product is analytics-heavy but weak on workflow management and compliance.

### Head-to-Head Comparison

| Dimension | PulseIQ | Veridian BioAnalytics |
|---|---|---|
| FDA 21 CFR Part 11 | Full compliance, validation docs included | Not compliant — explicitly not targeting regulated workflows |
| Implementation timeline | 6-10 weeks | 3-4 weeks (analytics only, limited scope) |
| AI-powered insights | Yes — sales + lab operations insights | Yes — lab analytics only |
| LIMS functionality | Full LIMS + analytics | Analytics layer only, requires separate LIMS |
| Pricing (startup biotech) | ~$2,000/month | ~$1,200/month |
| HL7/FHIR integration | Yes | No |
| Audit trail | Full immutable audit trail | Basic logging only |
| Validation documentation | Full IQ/OQ/PQ package | None available |

### Where We Win
- Any regulated workflow (clinical, GxP, submission-bound data)
- Organizations that need a full LIMS + analytics solution (not just analytics)
- Hospital systems and clinical-stage companies where HIPAA and HL7 matter
- Deals where the prospect asks about 21 CFR Part 11 at any point

### Where They Win
- Pre-IND research organizations with no regulatory requirements
- Genomics-only shops that just want data visualization
- Price-sensitive startups doing discovery research only

### Trap Questions to Ask the Prospect
- "What happens to your Veridian data when you enter clinical trials and need 21 CFR Part 11?"
- "Which LIMS will you use alongside Veridian, and what does that total cost look like?"
- "Have you asked them about their audit trail for regulatory submissions?"

### Displacement Strategy
Veridian often wins early-stage accounts, but those accounts outgrow them when they
enter clinical development. Position PulseIQ as the platform they'll need in 18 months —
and offer migration assistance as a proof point.

## Competitor 3: ClinexLab Suite

**Company overview:** ClinexLab is a clinical trial management software (CTMS) vendor
that has expanded into LIMS and lab analytics. Strong in hospital systems and academic
medical centers. Weak in commercial biotech and genomics.

### Head-to-Head Comparison

| Dimension | PulseIQ | ClinexLab Suite |
|---|---|---|
| FDA 21 CFR Part 11 | Full compliance | Compliant for clinical, gaps in research workflows |
| HL7/FHIR integration | Yes | Yes — this is their strength |
| Genomics/sequencing support | Strong | Minimal |
| Lab automation integrations | 40+ connectors | 12 connectors (clinical-focused) |
| AI insights | Yes — autonomous daily insights | Dashboards only, no AI layer |
| Pricing (enterprise) | ~$15,000/month | ~$18,000/month |
| Implementation timeline | 6-10 weeks | 12-20 weeks |
| Research workflow support | Strong | Weak — built for clinical, not research |

### Where We Win
- Commercial biotech, genomics, and research-stage organizations
- Any deal where lab automation and instrument integrations matter
- Organizations that want AI-powered insights rather than manual dashboards
- Mid-size pharma with both research and clinical workflows (we cover both)

### Where They Win
- Hospital systems already deep in the ClinexLab clinical ecosystem
- Academic medical centers with EHR integration requirements (Epic, Cerner)
- Deals driven by a clinical IT team rather than a lab or commercial team

### Trap Questions to Ask the Prospect
- "Does the decision include your research team, or just clinical IT?"
- "How does ClinexLab handle your sequencing and genomics data workflows?"
- "What is their AI roadmap — when do they expect to have automated insights?"

## General Competitive Guidance

**On pricing pressure:** Do not discount more than 15% without VP approval. Emphasize
total cost of ownership (implementation, validation docs, integrations) rather than
competing on monthly subscription price alone.

**On feature gaps:** If a prospect surfaces a feature we don't have, acknowledge it
directly. Never oversell. Log feature gaps in the CRM so product can track patterns.

**On reference requests:** We can offer customer reference calls for any competitive deal.
Request references through your CSM with 48 hours' notice. Do not provide references
without going through this process.
