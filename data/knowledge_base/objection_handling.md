# Objection Handling Guide

This guide covers the most common objections encountered in PulseIQ enterprise sales cycles
within the life sciences industry. Use these responses as starting points and adapt to the
specific context of each prospect.

---

## 1. "We're concerned about FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliance."

**Why it comes up:** Any software touching electronic records in a regulated lab environment
must comply with 21 CFR Part 11. Buyers in pharma and biotech are trained to ask this first.

**Response:**
PulseIQ is built with 21 CFR Part 11 compliance as a core architectural requirement, not an
afterthought. This includes full audit trails on all data modifications, role-based access
controls, electronic signature support, and time-stamped activity logs. We provide a
Validation Master Plan and IQ/OQ/PQ documentation to support your internal validation
process. Our compliance team can schedule a technical review call with your QA team at any
point in the sales cycle.

**Key proof points:**
- Pre-built validation documentation package included with enterprise tier
- Audit trail is immutable and exportable for regulatory submissions
- SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure

---

## 2. "Our IT security team has concerns about data security and HIPAA."

**Why it comes up:** Hospital systems and any org handling patient-adjacent data must
demonstrate HIPAA compliance. IT teams at research institutions and pharma companies have
strict vendor security review processes.

**Response:**
PulseIQ is HIPAA-compliant and undergoes annual third-party penetration testing. All data
is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We operate on SOC 2 Type II
certified infrastructure and can provide our full security documentation package, including
our Business Associate Agreement (BAA), for your IT review. Most customers complete their
security review within two to three weeks using our pre-filled security questionnaire.

**Key proof points:**
- BAA available for all tiers
- Data never leaves your designated cloud region
- SSO/SAML integration with major identity providers (Okta, Azure AD)
- Annual third-party pen testing with results available under NDA

---

## 3. "We don't have budget approved for this right now — we're mid-cycle."

**Why it comes up:** Research institutions and pharma companies typically run annual budget
cycles. Deals that surface outside of the budget planning window get pushed to the following
year unless there is a compelling event.

**Response:**
We hear this often, and we can work with your budget cycle. A few options: First, we can
structure a proof-of-concept engagement that fits within discretionary spend limits (often
under $25,000) so you can build the internal ROI case before the next budget cycle opens.
Second, we offer multi-year pricing that locks in current rates — useful if budget is being
planned now for next year. Third, if there's a regulatory deadline or commercial launch
driving urgency, we can help you build the business case for an out-of-cycle approval.

**Key proof points:**
- POC packages starting at $15,000 for 60-day engagements
- ROI calculator available showing average 23% reduction in sales cycle length
- Reference customers available who went through similar budget processes

---

## 4. "We're worried about integration with our existing lab systems (ELN, LIMS, instruments)."

**Why it comes up:** Life sciences organizations have complex, often fragmented technology
stacks. Integration failure is a top reason enterprise software implementations fail.

**Response:**
Integration is where we invest most heavily. PulseIQ supports HL7/FHIR for clinical data
exchange, REST APIs for ELN and LIMS connections, and pre-built connectors for the most
common platforms including Benchling, LabVantage, IDBS, and Waters instruments. For
custom integrations, our professional services team scopes and delivers the integration as
part of onboarding. We do not go live until integration testing passes your acceptance
criteria. Implementation timeline is typically 6-10 weeks depending on the number of
integrations.

**Key proof points:**
- Pre-built connectors for Benchling, LabVantage, IDBS, Thermo Fisher instruments
- REST API with full documentation for custom integrations
- Integration acceptance testing is a formal go-live gate
- 90-day post-launch support SLA included

## 5. "We had a bad experience with our last software implementation. We're risk-averse."

**Why it comes up:** Enterprise software implementations in life sciences frequently run
over time and over budget. Buyers who have been burned are skeptical of vendor promises.

**Response:**
That experience is more common than it should be, and it's a fair concern. Here's how we
reduce implementation risk: we use a phased rollout starting with a single team or
indication before expanding, we assign a dedicated Customer Success Manager for the first
12 months, and we guarantee go-live within the contracted timeline or we extend your
subscription at no cost. We can also connect you with three reference customers who had
similar concerns and can speak to their implementation experience directly.

**Key proof points:**
- 94% of implementations complete on or before contracted go-live date
- Dedicated CSM included in growth and enterprise tiers
- Go-live timeline guarantee in the contract
- Reference calls available within 48 hours of request

---

## 6. "Your competitor [X] offers the same thing for less."

**Why it comes up:** Prospects use competitive pricing to negotiate. Often they have not
fully evaluated the competing product or are comparing different feature sets.

**Response:**
We'd rather you choose the right tool than the cheapest one. A few things worth evaluating
side by side: 21 CFR Part 11 validation documentation (many competitors offer compliance
in name only), the depth of lab instrument integrations, and the total cost of
implementation including professional services. We've found that implementations with
competitors often require significant custom development that erases the initial price
difference. We're happy to walk through a feature-by-feature comparison — we have
battlecard materials for the most common alternatives.

**Key proof points:**
- See competitor battlecards for detailed comparisons
- Request a total cost of ownership analysis
- Ask competitors for their validation documentation package

---

## 7. "We need to see FDA submission-ready output before we can move forward."

**Why it comes up:** Pharma and clinical-stage biotech companies need to know that any
data captured in the system can be included in regulatory submissions without remediation.

**Response:**
PulseIQ generates submission-ready audit trails and data exports formatted to eCTD
standards. We include sample eCTD data packages in our enterprise documentation. Our
regulatory affairs team can conduct a review session to walk through submission workflows
specific to your indication. We also maintain a regulatory change log so you're informed
of any updates relevant to FDA or EMA requirements.

**Key proof points:**
- eCTD-formatted data exports available in enterprise tier
- Regulatory change log updated quarterly
- Free regulatory review session for enterprise prospects
